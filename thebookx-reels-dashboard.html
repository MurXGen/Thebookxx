Why this format works (and how to post it)

  • One repeatable look. Same white listicle + serif title every day builds instant recognition (the reference channel did exactly this to 54K followers).
  • Hook in the title. "8 Signs You Overthink Everything:" promises a payoff and makes people stop.
  • Red leads, black rest. The eye lands on the bold red phrase first, then reads on — high retention per slide.
  • Last line = the ask. Point 8 is always a follow-CTA, and the footer always says Grab books at ₹1.
  • Trending audio + daily cadence. Static image, fresh audio, posted every day — the algorithm rewards consistency and saves.
  • Anchor + rotation. The Art of Clarity returns every other day with a new angle (overthinking / decisions / rhymes), so the hero book compounds without feeling repetitive.
Caption first 2 lines carry the hook. Ask for a Save and a comment. Reply to early comments fast.
